About

The short version

I graduated from Taibah University as a First Honors graduate with a GPA of 4.8, specializing in Security and Networks. In my second year, I placed first in a HackerRank secure coding competition my professor organized for our Programming 1 class, competing against classmates to write the fastest, most secure solutions. That competitive edge is what pulled me toward security: I like the puzzle of figuring out where a system is weakest before someone else does.

During my internship at Saudi Paramount Computer Systems, I shadowed an active SOC team through real-time event monitoring, threat classification, and incident triage, helped assess Shadow IT and unauthorized devices to shrink the attack surface, and got hands-on with CCNA-framework network fundamentals, firewall and VPN configuration, and Active Directory identity management.

I've since gone further into offense-informed defense through a Penetration Testing Fundamentals program at Tuwaiq Academy, working with tools like Nmap, Metasploit, Burp Suite, and Wireshark. I've trained with nationally recognized entities including the NCA, SITE, KAUST, and Tuwaiq Academy, and I'm currently studying for CompTIA Security+ (scheduled for August 2026). Beyond SOC work, I'm drawn to security broadly, network defense, penetration testing, and the kind of detail-oriented analysis that catches what a quick glance would miss.
