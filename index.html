How I write up an alert

  1. AlertWhat fired, on which host, and when.
  2. EvidenceThe logs and traffic that support or contradict it.
  3. VerdictTrue positive, false positive, or escalate, with the reason.
  4. ResponseContainment and follow-up steps for the team.
This is the format of my SIEM case studies, which are in progress.
